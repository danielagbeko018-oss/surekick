SureKick Straight Win

Predicts the result from football facts only: every team's results this season, adjusted for opponent strength, home advantage, recent form and injuries. The odds are used afterward, only to judge value. Any odds are allowed. Nothing here is a guarantee, and most value picks still lose more often than they win.

How the colors work

Green: the best value pick. The file's chance beats the break-even rate of the odds by 6 points or more, confidence is not low, team news is all Clear, and no Major injury. It is not safe.

Yellow: a likely winner with a thinner edge (3 to 6 points), or an edge that still has something unchecked, such as team news.

Red: no clear edge at these odds. Grey: no odds entered yet, so only the chances are shown.

Only one side of a match can be green. Break-even rate is 100 divided by the odds.

Find a match

Nine leagues are rated from a full season of results. The Champions League, European Championship and World Cup are also available, with lower reliability. For other competitions, use the hand-entry box.

    Injuries by area

    None: no key player missing. Minor: 1 key player out. Major: 2 or more out, or the top scorer or first-choice goalkeeper when the backup is clearly weaker. Midfield counts for half as much. A Major also counts as a team news concern.

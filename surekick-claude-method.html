SureKick Claude Method

Pick an upcoming match, or type the results by hand. It estimates each market's chance, compares it with the odds you can get, and only calls a bet when there is a clear edge. Most matches should be skipped. It does not use Claude, so it has no daily limit, and nothing here is a guarantee.

How this method works

1. Estimate the chance. A goals model (recent matches count more, injuries adjust it) is blended with how often each team's recent results hit the market: 70% model, 30% recent form.

2. Compare with the odds. A bet needs the chance to beat the break-even rate by 5 points or more, or 8 points when the odds are below 1.40.

3. Check confidence. Small samples, weak opposition, injuries, and a model that disagrees with recent form all lower it. Low confidence blocks a bet.

4. Team news must be checked. All three team news boxes must be Clear. Unknown or Concern blocks a bet.

5. One bet per match. Only the market with the biggest edge can be a bet candidate.

6. Stake small. The suggested stake is a quarter of the Kelly amount, never above your maximum stake.

These rules are my own starting approach. They have not been tested, so judge them by your logged results.

    Injuries by area

    None: no key player missing. Minor: 1 key player out. Major: 2 or more out, or the main goalscorer or first-choice goalkeeper when the backup is clearly weaker. These lower that team's expected goals in the model. A Major also counts as a team news concern.

    Model settings

    The percentages are starting estimates, not tested figures. Change them if your results show they are too small or too large.

    Odds (needed for a verdict)

    Enter the decimal odds you can get for the markets you are considering. Without odds the tool cannot call a bet. Fill in your own chance only if you want to replace the tool's estimate.

    Bankroll and staking

    Use any currency. A small fixed share of your bankroll per bet (1% to 2%) stops a losing run from wiping you out.

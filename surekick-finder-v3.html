SureKick Match Finder

Pick an upcoming match and it pulls results from the free football-data.org service, then screens all ten markets with Rulebook v2.0. It does not use Claude, so it has no daily limit. Screening is not a guarantee, and it does not tell you whether a bet has value.

    Odds (optional)

    Enter decimal odds to see the break-even probability. Add your own probability estimate to compare. The tool never makes up an estimate from past hit rates.

    Bankroll and staking

    Use any currency. A small fixed share of your bankroll per bet (1% to 2%) stops a losing run from wiping you out.

    Match log

    Save each analyzed match, add the bets you actually place (odds and stake), then add the final score. Profit and hit rates update below, with the number of bets beside each.
